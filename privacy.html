Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

expense-backups is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

  • It uses the Google Drive permission drive.file. It can see only the files it creates, never the rest of your Drive.
  • It stores encrypted backup files in a folder in the owner's Google Drive. File names and contents are encrypted before upload.
  • It collects no data from anyone, shares nothing with third parties, and has no analytics or advertising.
  • You can revoke its access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
